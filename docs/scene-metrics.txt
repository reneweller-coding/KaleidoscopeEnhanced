Scene metrics -- catalogue baseline, measured 2026-08-21

How this was produced
---------------------
  Tools\scan_scenes.ps1 -All -Seconds 8 -Out scan
  python Tools\scene_metrics.py Release\scan --json Tools\scene_metrics.json

Conditions, all of which matter for comparing against a later run:
  * renderScale pinned to 1.0 and autoScale OFF. With the adaptive scaler on,
    an expensive scene keeps its fps up by dropping resolution, so fast and
    slow scenes both report "fine".
  * ONE pinned background photo, the median-brightness image of the 2813 in
    the collection (Tools\pick_scan_image.py). Scenes that fold the photo
    inherit its brightness; with a random photo per run the worst luma delta
    between two runs was 0.43 and only 4 of 10 flag verdicts reproduced. With
    the photo pinned: 0.002 and 9 of 10.
  * fps is the APP's own report (KALEIDO_FPS_LOG), not frames-on-disk divided
    by seconds. The recorder writes constant-rate video with duplicate fill,
    so counting frames now says nothing about scene cost.
  * Eight scenes were re-measured after bug fixes, because their earlier rows
    described a broken render: TunnelPlain and TunnelReverse (a uniform whose
    upload the driver rejected, see Tools\check_uniform_types.py) and the six
    raymarchers that read an uninitialised hit position.
