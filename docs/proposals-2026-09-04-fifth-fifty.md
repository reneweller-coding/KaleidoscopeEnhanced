# Fifty more scenes (proposed 04.09.2026, fifth set)

Themes chosen against the 816 scenes in the catalogue after v1.13.0: none
of the subjects below exists yet -- no camera obscura, welding arc, stage
rig, X-ray scanner, thermal camera, photoelastic fringes, eclipse
crescents, light painting, laser harp, crepuscular rays, split-flap board,
escalator, transit map, suspension bridge, glasshouse, server room, wind
turbine, heliostat, aqueduct, cooling tower, tide pool, spillway,
travertine terrace, mangrove, puddle, water strider, salmon run, cenote,
rice terrace, pivot irrigation, knitting, quilt, bobbin lace, batik,
kintsugi, embroidery, kumiko, chainmail, cloisonne, damascus, metronome,
hourglass, sundial, slot machine, Galton board, music box, wind chime,
step sequencer, abacus or jukebox scene.  Every scene follows the standing
rules: camera still or steadily moving on the scene clock, fast envelopes
only on light and colour, discrete counts once per activation, round
particles, the drop the only cut, and every gate on an envelope a
smoothstep.  Pipeline in brackets; audio mapping after the dash.

## A. Light and optics
1. CameraObscuraRoom [2D] -- a dark room, the world outside (the photo) projected upside-down and soft through a pinhole onto the far wall; the aperture opens with the swell (brighter and softer) and closes (darker and sharper), dust motes (round) drift in the light cone on the clock; the kick is a flicker of the daylight outside.
2. WeldingArcSeam [2D] -- a torch running a steel seam on the clock: the blinding arc core, round spark showers, the weld bead cooling from white to blue behind; the visor tint deepens with the level (light only); the photo is the workshop.
3. StageRigMovingHeads [2D] -- a concert rig in haze: moving-head beams sweeping on the clock, one colour per chroma class, gobos of the photo on the floor; the kick is a short flash on the backline, never a full-frame strobe.
4. XrayLuggageBelt [2D] -- a security scanner: bags gliding on the belt on the clock, drawn in the false-colour X-ray palette (orange organic, green mixed, blue metal) with the photo as their contents; the bands set the density; the kick a threat highlight (light).
5. ThermalIronbowStreet [2D] -- a street through a thermal camera: the photo in the ironbow palette, warm bodies as soft blobs walking on the clock, the ambient heat on the swell, the kick a hot flash from a doorway.
6. PhotoelasticStressFringes [2D] -- a transparent part between crossed polarisers: rainbow contour fringes around a load that presses on the swell (slow), the fringe order rising with the bass, the photo as the lit background; the fringes rotate slowly on the clock.
7. EclipseLeafCrescents [2D] -- dappled light through a canopy during a partial eclipse: every gap projects a crescent that thins over the arc, the leaves sway on the clock (light and shadow only); the photo is the ground.
8. LightPaintingTrails [2D] -- long-exposure light painting: glowing trails drawn by wands over the dark photo, persisting and fading on the clock, colours by chroma class, the kick a brief silhouette of the dancer.
9. LaserHarpBeams [2D] -- vertical laser beams in haze, one per chroma class, brightening as their class sounds; a hand (round shadow) crosses the beams on the clock and each crossed beam blooms; the photo is the stage floor.
10. CrepuscularRaysForest [2D] -- god rays through mist in a forest: each ray a spectrum band, the mist drifting on the clock, the sun sinking over the arc, the photo as the trees.

## B. Built spaces

12. EscalatorHallCrossing [indirect] -- a hall of criss-crossing escalators moving on the clock, riders as round-head silhouettes, photo panels on the walls; the steps light by band, the hall light on the swell.
13. SubwayMapPulse [2D] -- a transit map: lines as coloured polylines, one per chroma class, trains as pulses travelling on the clock, stations lighting when their class sounds; the photo is the paper.
14. SuspensionBridgeCables [indirect] -- under the deck of a suspension bridge: cables and hangers converging, lamps along them by band, fog on the swell; the crossing is steady on the clock.
15. GlasshouseIronRibs [2D] -- a Victorian glasshouse: iron ribs and panes, the photo as the garden beyond, condensation drops (round) sliding on the clock, the sun sweeping over the arc, the warm haze on the bass.
16. ServerRoomAisle [2D] -- a data-centre aisle: rack after rack with LED columns blinking by band, cable trays overhead, cold-aisle fog on the swell, the photo as rack labels; a steady walk on the clock.
17. WindTurbineFieldDusk [indirect] -- turbines turning on the clock, each at its own steady rate, the photo as the dusk sky, nav lights breathing with the kick (light); the wind is the swell, on the light only.
18. HeliostatSolarTower [2D] -- a field of heliostat mirrors reflecting the sky (photo), tilting slowly on the clock, the tower receiver glowing with the bass, glints on the treble.
19. AqueductArchesValley [2D] -- a Roman aqueduct across a valley: arches lit by chroma class, water in the channel on the clock, birds (round) crossing; the photo is the valley.
20. CoolingTowerPlumes [2D] -- hyperboloid towers with steam plumes rising on the clock, the plumes lit by the bands, the amount on the swell; the photo is the evening sky.

## E. Rhythm machines and toys

47. WindChimeTubes [2D] -- wind chimes: tubes swaying gently on the clock, one per class, glowing as their class sounds, the porch (photo) behind; the kick a clapper glint.
48. StepSequencerGrid [2D] -- a step sequencer: the playhead sweeping on the clock, cells lit by chroma and band, the photo as the cell faces; the kick lights the current column (a grid by design, not a particle field).
49. AbacusBeadCount [2D] -- an abacus counting on the clock: beads sliding on rods, one rod per band, bead brightness by band energy; the photo is the frame wood.
50. JukeboxBubbleTubes [2D] -- a Wurlitzer: bubble tubes with round bubbles rising on the clock, colour cycling with the chroma, the record carousel turning steadily, the photo as the labels; the neon on the swell.
