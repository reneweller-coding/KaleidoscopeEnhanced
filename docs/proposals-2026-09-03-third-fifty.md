# Fifty more scenes (proposed 03.09.2026, third set)

Themes chosen against the 827 scenes already in the catalogue: nothing here
repeats an existing subject (tokamak, ferrofluid, schlieren, Chladni,
Lichtenberg, mycelium, coral, capsid, pulsar, wormhole, tesseract, moire,
Rayleigh-Taylor, Meissner, cloaking, torus knot, radiolarian and wave
packets are all taken).  Every scene follows the standing rules: camera
still or steadily moving on the scene clock, fast envelopes only on light
and colour, discrete counts once per activation, round particles, the drop
the only cut.  Pipeline in brackets; audio mapping after the dash.
