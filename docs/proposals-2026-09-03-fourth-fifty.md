# Fifty more scenes (proposed 03.09.2026, fourth set)

Themes chosen against the 877 scenes now in the catalogue (the first three
fifties included): none of the subjects below exists yet -- no haboob,
permafrost, salt flat, calving, iceberg, noctilucent cloud, hurricane eye,
sun dog, mirage, seismograph, foundry, glassblowing, kiln, orrery, forge,
candle, Ferris wheel, balloon, regatta, marble run, peacock, termite,
monarch, glow-worm, turtle, penguin, blossom front, bamboo, superbloom,
spore, ukiyo-e, manuscript, zellij, rangoli, calligraphy, fresco, domino,
spirograph, kaleidocycle, tangram, piano roll, vinyl, tape, modular synth,
tuning fork, formant, granular, sidechain, railway or silence scene.
Every scene follows the standing rules: camera still or steadily moving on
the scene clock, fast envelopes only on light and colour, discrete counts
once per activation, round particles, the drop the only cut, and every
gate on an envelope a smoothstep.  Pipeline in brackets; audio mapping
after the dash.

## A. Earth and weather
1. HaboobDustWall [2D] -- a wall of dust rolling over a desert town; the wall advances on the scene clock, its height on the swell, lightning inside it on the kick; the photo is the town it swallows.
2. PermafrostPolygons [patches] -- patterned ground from above: ice-wedge polygons with meltwater ponds mirroring the sky (photo); the ponds brighten with the bass, frost glints on the treble.
3. SalarMirrorFlats [2D] -- the salt flat after rain: a perfect mirror of the sky (photo), the hexagonal salt crust just under the water; the swell is the cloud light, walkers as round silhouettes on the clock.
4. GlacierCalvingFront [indirect] -- an ice cliff over the sea; blocks calve on continuous phases (each block a photo slab tumbling into the water, splash as round spray), the bass lights the blue ice from within.
5. IcebergWaterline [2D] -- an iceberg seen half above, half below the waterline; the underwater mass is the photo refracted in blue; light rays on the swell, bubbles (round) rising on the clock.
6. NoctilucentClouds [2D] -- the electric-blue night clouds at the edge of space: rippled sheets drifting on the clock over a dark landscape; the treble is the ripple detail, the swell the twilight.
7. HurricaneEyeWall [2D] -- inside the eye: a stadium of cloud wall rising around, the calm sky above; the wall rotates steadily, its height on the swell, lightning in it on the kick; the photo is the cloud texture.
8. SundogHaloSky [2D] -- a low sun with the 22 degree halo, sun dogs and the parhelic circle; the halo brightness is the treble (ice crystals), the sun the swell; the photo is the winter landscape.
9. FataMorganaMirage [2D] -- a ship on the horizon stretched and stacked by a mirage: the photo drawn in inverted, wobbling slices that breathe on the clock; the swell is the heat.
10. SeismographDrum [2D] -- a rotating drum with the pen writing the level as a trace: the drum turns on the clock, the trace is the recent loudness, a tremor on the kick is an ink spike (light on the pen), the bass shakes only the trace, never the frame.

## B. Craft and machines
11. FoundryPour [2D] -- molten metal poured into a mould: the melt as a bright stream on the clock, sparks as round particles, the glow the bass; the photo is the foundry floor.
12. GlassblowerGather [2D] -- a gather of glass on the pipe inflating on the swell, turning on the clock, the colour cooling from orange to the photo; the kick is the reheat flash.
13. KilnGlazeCrystals [2D] -- a fired bowl whose glaze grows crystal flowers over the arc (crystalline glaze), each crystal lit by a chroma class; the photo is the glaze body.
14. OrreryBrass [indirect] -- a brass orrery: planets on arms turning at Kepler rates on the clock, the sun lamp the bass, the photo on the planet spheres.
15. BlacksmithSparks [2D] -- a glowing bar on the anvil; the kick is the hammer blow as a burst of round sparks (light) and a flash, the swell the bar heat; the photo is the smithy.
16. CandleForestBreath [2D] -- hundreds of candles; the flames lean together in the draught (a slow wave on the clock), their brightness the bands, the kick a gutter; the photo is the wax.
17. FerrisWheelNight [indirect] -- a Ferris wheel of photo cabins turning on the clock, its lights running patterns with the bands; the fair below the swell.
18. HotAirBalloonDawn [indirect] -- a fleet of balloons rising at dawn, each a photo envelope, burners flaring on the kick (light), the sunrise on the swell.
19. RegattaSpinnakers [indirect] -- yachts with spinnakers of the photo heeling in the wind (the swell), the sea of the bass; the fleet sails steadily on the clock.
20. MarbleRunTrack [2D] -- marbles (round) rolling a looping track on the clock, each marble a chroma class lighting the rail it rides; the kick a bell at the bottom.

## C. Nature and animals

22. TermiteMoundPlumes [2D] -- a mound at dusk with heat plumes (schlieren-like refraction of the photo) rising on the clock; the bass is the mound's warmth.
23. MonarchRoostTree [indirect] -- thousands of monarchs on a fir, wings opening with the sun (the swell) and closing; a few take off on continuous phases; the photo is the wing pattern.
24. GlowwormCaveThreads [2D] -- the cave ceiling of blue-green threads with beads (round), glowing with the bands, the water below mirroring; the kick a drip.
25. TurtleHatchlingsMoon [indirect] -- hatchlings (round backs) crawling to the moonlit sea on the clock, the moon the swell, the waves the bass.
26. PenguinHuddleRotation [indirect] -- a huddle in the blizzard, slowly rotating (the outer birds working inward) on the clock; snow as round flakes; the swell is the storm.
27. CherryBlossomFront [2D] -- the blossom front sweeping across a map-like landscape over the arc, petals (round) drifting on the clock; the photo is the land.
28. BambooGroveWind [2D] -- tall culms swaying in a slow wave on the clock, light through the leaves on the swell; the photo is the light beyond.
29. SuperbloomOpening [2D] -- a desert of flowers opening with the swell (petals unfold smoothly), colours from chroma classes; the kick a bee.
30. SporeReleaseGills [2D] -- a mushroom cap from below: gills radiating, spores (round) drifting down on the clock, lit by the treble; the photo is the cap.

## D. Culture and play

32. IlluminatedManuscript [2D] -- a page with a gilded initial and vine borders; the gold catches light on the swell, the vines grow over the arc, the photo is the miniature.
33. ZellijTilework [2D] -- Moroccan zellij: an eight-fold star pattern of photo tiles; a slow rotation of the pattern on the clock, tiles lit by chroma.
34. RangoliPowderMandala [2D] -- coloured powder laid down in a radial pattern over the arc, grains as round dots; the kick sprinkles more.
35. CalligraphyBrushStrokes [2D] -- brush strokes drawn on the clock across the photo as paper, ink bleeding on the swell, pressure from the bass (stroke width, slow).
36. FrescoRestorationReveal [2D] -- a soot-darkened fresco; the restorer's cleaning reveals the photo in patches over the arc; the swell the lamp.
37. DominoCascadeWave [2D] -- a field of dominoes toppling in a wave on the clock (each tile a smooth rotation), tiles of the photo; the kick lights the falling front.
38. SpirographGearDraw [2D] -- a spirograph pen drawing hypotrochoids on the clock, radii from the chroma classes (once per activation), the trace fading; the photo is the paper.
39. KaleidocycleFold [indirect] -- a kaleidocycle (a ring of tetrahedra turning inside out) rotating on the clock, its faces the photo; bands light the faces.
40. TangramShuffle [2D] -- seven tangram pieces of the photo gliding between figures over the arc (positions blended smoothly); the kick lights the piece that has just landed.

## E. Music and sound
41. PianoRollWaterfall [2D, audioMelody] -- notes falling as bars onto a keyboard: the melody history as the bars, chroma classes as the keys lit; the kick lights the hammer.
42. VinylGrooveMicroscope [2D] -- the groove under a microscope, the stylus riding it: the wall shape is the waveform (audioWave), the groove scrolls on the clock, dust as round motes.
43. TapeReelEcho [2D] -- two reels turning at speeds on the clock, the tape carrying the photo as its oxide, echoes as repeated dim copies of the spectrum along the tape path.
44. ModularSynthPatchbay [2D] -- a wall of modules: knobs turning slowly, patch cables lit by the bands they carry, the oscilloscope showing the waveform; the photo is the panel graphics.
45. TuningForkChoir [2D] -- twelve forks, one per chroma class, each ringing as a standing envelope (no vibration drawn) at its class energy; the kick strikes the loudest.
46. FormantVowelSpace [2D] -- the vowel chart (F1 vs F2) with a marker gliding to where the spectral shape points (from the centroid and spread, slow), vowels as photo blobs.
47. GranularCloudSynth [2D] -- grains (round) scattered over the spectrogram on the clock, each grain a snippet of the photo, density on the swell, pitch on the y axis.
48. SidechainLungBreath [2D] -- a pair of lungs breathing with the swell (inflate) and the kick (the pump ducking as a light dip), alveoli as the photo.
49. ChordRailwaySwitches [2D] -- a railway yard: the train runs on the clock, the switches set by the sounding chord (chroma) route it, signals lit by the classes; the photo is the landscape.
50. SilenceSnowfallHush [2D] -- a quiet street: when the music is quiet, snow (round flakes) settles and the scene whitens; loud passages melt it (slow, via the swell); the photo is the street.
