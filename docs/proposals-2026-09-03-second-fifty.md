# Fifty more scenes (proposed 03.09.2026, second set)

All under the standing rules: V7c (every motion continuous), V7d (no whole-frame
motion on fast envelopes; the camera stands or travels steadily), V8e (round
particles). Names checked against the 666-scene catalogue.

## A. Space
1. DarkNebulaBokGlobules -- dust cocoons before an emission nebula (the photo); stars behind dim by real column density; swell thickens, onsets ignite protostars inside.
2. TidalDisruptionEvent -- a star torn into a stream by a black hole (state-buffer sim); the arc is the fly-by, the drop the tearing (light).
3. VacuumDecayBubble -- a bubble of true vacuum rewriting the picture inside; the wall grows with the build-up, inside/outside blend continuously.
4. PlanetaryNebulaShells -- concentric shells of a dying star, each a spectrum band, flown through on a log-periodic (seamless) zoom.
5. SolarEclipseTotality -- diamond ring and corona streamers from audioSpectrum[32]; dayPhase moves the moon.
6. IoVolcanicPlumes -- Jupiter fills the sky, plumes on Io rise as objects, sulphur colours from the palette.
7. MicrolensingCausticSweep -- a caustic network sweeps a star field; stars flare when a fold passes; swell = lens mass.
8. CosmicMicrowaveBackgroundSky -- the CMB sphere; multipoles glow with the bands (the acoustic peaks are sound).
9. WhiteHoleFountain -- photos well up out of the horizon and fly away; swell = fountain speed, kick = horizon flash.
10. OrbitalSunrise -- sunrise from orbit on dayPhase (sin/cos, never a wrap), city lights fade, valence = clouds.

## B. Endless zooms and tunnels

12. BookPagesTunnel -- a tunnel of turning pages, page turns as continuous rotations on the bar.
13. SpiralStairwellDescent -- endless descent down a stairwell, steps carry the photo.
14. CapillaryFlight -- through a capillary with cells streaming; the pulse is light in the wall.
15. TemporalZoomSSM -- the song zooms into itself: the SSM on nested time scales, exactly self-similar.
16. PowersOfTenZoom -- moon, planet, system, galaxy, cluster: a log-periodic scale ladder.
17. KarmanVortexStreet -- flight through a vortex street; onsets shed vortices (objects).
18. PapercutShadowBox -- layered paper cut-outs with parallax, backlit; chroma colours the layers.
19. LighthouseFresnelLens -- inside a Fresnel lens: rotating prism rings, the photo refracted behind.
20. GlacierCrevasseDescent -- into a blue crevasse; audioHigh = crystal glint, sub-bass = light.
